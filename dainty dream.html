Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp us on 0110 091 056.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within 1 to 3 days of confirmation. Personalised cases are printed to order, so please allow an extra 2 to 4 days.

My phone model isn't listed. Can I still order?

Yes, in many cases. Send us your exact phone model on WhatsApp or by email and we'll let you know which designs are available.

How much protection do the cases give?

Every case has shock-absorbing corners and a raised edge around the camera and screen, so your phone is protected from everyday drops and scratches.

How do I pay?

Once you send your order, we reply with your total and payment options. Your order is packed as soon as payment is confirmed.

What if my case doesn't fit or arrives damaged?

Contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll replace it free of charge. Personalised cases can be replaced only if there is a printing error.
